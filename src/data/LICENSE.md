# Data licenses

The files in this folder are generated by `scripts/build-data.mjs` from two open datasets.
They are adaptations of those datasets and are shared under the same licenses.
These licenses cover the data only. They do not cover the application code.

## glyphs.json — stroke order

- **Source:** [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright © 2009–present Ulrich Apel.
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
  You may use, share and adapt it if you credit the source and share adaptations under the same license.
- **Changes made:**
  - Kept only the stroke paths of the hiragana, katakana and kanji that the app uses.
  - Converted each SVG path, including its curves, into a polyline.
  - Simplified it with the Ramer–Douglas–Peucker method.
  - Scaled the coordinates from KanjiVG's 109-unit box to integers from 0 to 1000.
  - Dropped stroke numbers, element groupings and other metadata.

## kanji.json — kanji readings and meanings

- **Source:** [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project), the property of the
  [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/) (EDRDG).
- **License:** used in conformance with the Group's [licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), under
  [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Changes made:**
  - Kept only the kanji marked as old JLPT level 4 (about today's N5).
  - From each kanji, kept the character, stroke count, on and kun readings (affix-only kun readings removed) and up to four English meanings.
  - Sorted by school grade, stroke count and frequency.
  - The app hides meanings that only name a radical and shows at most three meanings.
